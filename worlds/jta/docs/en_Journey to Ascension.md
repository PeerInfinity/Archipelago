# Journey to Ascension

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What is Journey to Ascension?

Journey to Ascension is an incremental/idle game where you progress through zones by completing tasks, leveling skills,
and collecting perks. This randomizer shuffles perk placements across tasks, creating a new challenge each seed.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Perks that would normally be earned from specific tasks are shuffled into the Archipelago item pool. When you complete
a task in-game, you send out whatever item was placed at that location. The perks you need to progress may come from
other players in the multiworld.

A post-generation cost adjustment algorithm ensures the randomized seed remains completable by modifying task costs
based on the new perk placement order.

## What is the goal of Journey to Ascension?

The goal is to reach the configured goal zone. The default goal zone is zone 15. Once you reach it, your game is
complete and any remaining items in your world become accessible to other players.
